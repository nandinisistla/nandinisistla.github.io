About

I'm a product designer working across research, interface and physical form. A coat, a flood poster and an investing app turn out to be the same problem in different clothes: someone has a need they can't quite name, and the work is finding it first.

Right now I own end-to-end UI and UX at Catalyst Intro, an early-stage platform opening pre-IPO startups to everyday investors. Before that, an emergency flood protocol shaped by interviews with Hurricane Sandy survivors, and a coat co-created with the person who'd wear it. Accessibility isn't a checklist for me, it's usually where the interesting problem is.

I'm also at Columbia studying Industrial Engineering and Operations Research, which keeps me honest about the gap between what people say and what they actually do.

Outside of that I make art and take far too many pictures of sunsets, ducks, and anything else cute enough to justify stopping for. I also run a food page, where I photograph the meal properly, take one bite, and announce that I'm full.
